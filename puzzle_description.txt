LOGIC PUZZLE
==================================================

There are 5 positions (numbered 1 to 5).
Each position has a unique combination of:
  - Person: Alice, Bob, Carol, David, Emma
  - Color: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White
  - Drink: Coffee, Tea, Milk, Juice, Water
  - Pet: Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Rabbit
  - Profession: Doctor, Engineer, Teacher, Artist, Lawyer

CLUES:
1. David has a Cat.
2. Bob drinks Tea.
3. Bob has a Bird.
4. The person in position 3 does NOT drink Coffee.
5. The person who drinks Milk lives in the middle position (position 3).
6. The Teacher lives in the Red house.
7. The person in position 5 has a Dog.
8. Carol has a Fish.
9. Carol drinks Milk.
10. Emma drinks Coffee.
11. The Artist lives in the Green house.
12. The White house is immediately to the left of the Blue house.
13. The David lives in position 1.
14. The Lawyer lives exactly two positions to the left of the Teacher.
15. Emma has a Rabbit.
16. The Doctor lives in the White house.

QUESTION: Who drinks Water?
