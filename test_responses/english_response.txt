# Conference Presentation Schedule Puzzle - Solution

## Step-by-Step Reasoning Process

### Given Information
- **Researchers:** Dr. Anderson, Dr. Brown, Dr. Chen, Dr. Dubois, Dr. Evans
- **Time Slots:** 9:00 AM, 10:30 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:30 PM, 4:00 PM
- **Constraints:** 8 conditions to satisfy

### Solving Process

#### Step 1: Determine Dr. Chen's Time Slot

From **Constraint 8**: Dr. Chen presents in the morning (before 1:00 PM)
- Possible slots: 9:00 AM or 10:30 AM

From **Constraint 2**: Dr. Chen cannot present at 9:00 AM or 4:00 PM

**Combining these constraints: Dr. Chen must present at 10:30 AM**

#### Step 2: Determine Dr. Evans's Time Slot

From **Constraint 4**: Dr. Evans immediately follows Dr. Chen (consecutive slots)
- Since Dr. Chen is at 10:30 AM, Dr. Evans must be at 1:00 PM

#### Step 3: Verify Constraint 5

From **Constraint 5**: The 1:00 PM slot is occupied by someone whose name starts with a vowel
- Dr. Evans is at 1:00 PM
- "Evans" starts with 'E', which is a vowel ✓

#### Step 4: Determine Dr. Dubois's Time Slot

From **Constraint 3**: Dr. Dubois presents exactly two time slots after Dr. Chen
- Dr. Chen is at 10:30 AM (slot 2)
- Two slots later is 2:30 PM (slot 4)

**Dr. Dubois presents at 2:30 PM**

#### Step 5: Determine Dr. Anderson's Time Slot

From **Constraint 7**: There is exactly one presentation between Dr. Anderson and Dr. Evans
- Dr. Evans is at 1:00 PM (slot 3)
- For exactly one presentation between them, Dr. Anderson must be at slot 1 (9:00 AM) with Chen at slot 2, OR at slot 5 (4:00 PM) with someone at slot 4

From **Constraint 1**: Dr. Anderson must present before Dr. Brown
- This means Anderson comes earlier in the schedule than Brown

If Anderson were at 4:00 PM, Brown would have no valid slot after Anderson.
**Therefore, Dr. Anderson must be at 9:00 AM**

#### Step 6: Determine Dr. Brown's Time Slot

By elimination, **Dr. Brown presents at 4:00 PM**

#### Step 7: Verify All Constraints

Let me verify this solution against all 8 constraints:

1. ✓ Anderson (9:00 AM) before Brown (4:00 PM), and they are NOT consecutive (slots 1 and 5)
2. ✓ Chen (10:30 AM) is not at 9:00 AM or 4:00 PM
3. ✓ Dubois (2:30 PM, slot 4) is exactly two slots after Chen (10:30 AM, slot 2)
4. ✓ Evans (1:00 PM, slot 3) immediately follows Chen (10:30 AM, slot 2)
5. ✓ 1:00 PM is occupied by Evans, whose name starts with a vowel (E)
6. ✓ Brown (4:00 PM) presents in the afternoon
7. ✓ Exactly one presentation (Chen at 10:30 AM) between Anderson (9:00 AM) and Evans (1:00 PM)
8. ✓ Chen (10:30 AM) presents in the morning

All constraints are satisfied!

---

## FINAL ANSWER - Complete Schedule

**9:00 AM:** Dr. Anderson
**10:30 AM:** Dr. Chen
**1:00 PM:** Dr. Evans
**2:30 PM:** Dr. Dubois
**4:00 PM:** Dr. Brown

## Additional Challenge: If Dr. Dubois Cancels

**Question:** Which researcher could take Dr. Dubois's slot (2:30 PM) without violating any of the other constraints about their own scheduling (excluding constraint 3)?

### Analysis of Each Researcher

**Dr. Anderson (currently at 9:00 AM):**
- Constraint 1: Anderson must be before Brown and not consecutive
  - If Anderson moves to 2:30 PM and Brown stays at 4:00 PM: still before Brown, not consecutive ✓
- Constraint 7: Exactly one presentation between Anderson and Evans
  - Anderson at 2:30 PM, Evans at 1:00 PM: they would be consecutive (zero between them) ✗

**VERDICT: Cannot take the slot (violates constraint 7)**

---

**Dr. Brown (currently at 4:00 PM):**
- Constraint 1: Anderson must be before Brown and not consecutive
  - Anderson at 9:00 AM, Brown at 2:30 PM: Anderson is before Brown, not consecutive ✓
- Constraint 6: Brown presents in the afternoon
  - 2:30 PM is in the afternoon ✓

**VERDICT: CAN take the slot (all constraints satisfied)**

---

**Dr. Chen (currently at 10:30 AM):**
- Constraint 2: Chen cannot be at 9:00 AM or 4:00 PM
  - 2:30 PM is neither ✓
- Constraint 4: Evans immediately follows Chen
  - Evans at 1:00 PM, Chen at 2:30 PM: Evans does NOT follow Chen ✗
- Constraint 8: Chen presents in the morning
  - 2:30 PM is afternoon, not morning ✗

**VERDICT: Cannot take the slot (violates constraints 4 and 8)**

---

**Dr. Evans (currently at 1:00 PM):**
- Constraint 4: Evans immediately follows Chen
  - Chen at 10:30 AM, Evans at 2:30 PM: NOT consecutive ✗
- Constraint 7: Exactly one presentation between Anderson and Evans
  - Anderson at 9:00 AM, Evans at 2:30 PM: Chen at 10:30 AM is between them (exactly one) ✓

**VERDICT: Cannot take the slot (violates constraint 4)**

---
